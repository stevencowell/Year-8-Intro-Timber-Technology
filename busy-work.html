BUSY WORK

Practice that helps the project.

These short activities build the language, drawing and design habits you need. Your responses save on this device.

ACTIVITY 01

Safety decision path

A classmate is about to use a machine without checking first. Choose the best response.

ACTIVITY 02

Tool-function match

Match each broad tool category to its purpose. Use your class tool board and teacher guidance for the real tools.

ACTIVITY 03

Pine property case file

Use timber vocabulary accurately. Describe the visible grain, density and knot features of a timber sample provided in class.

ACTIVITY 04

Manufactured board process map

Choose one product — plywood, OSB, LVL, CLT or wood-plastic composite — then explain the wood form used and one useful property.

ACTIVITY 05

Scale measurement lab

Open the original two-page plan. Find one written dimension and explain why it is safer to use that number than measure a screen preview.

Open larger (PDF, 2 pages)

ACTIVITY 06

Third-angle drawing check

In your own words, explain what information you would look for in a plan, front elevation and right-hand elevation.

ACTIVITY 07

Isometric drawing challenge

On paper or in your folio, sketch a simple box-like form in isometric. Then explain one way it is different from a third-angle drawing.

ACTIVITY 08

Emblem design decision

List three different thumbnail ideas, then choose one using personal meaning, clarity and balance as your criteria.

ACTIVITY 09

Production quality log

When your teacher asks, record a dated observation, the action you took and your next step. Do not make up practical steps.

ACTIVITY 10

Function and aesthetics evaluation

Write one observation about function and one about appearance. Finish with one realistic improvement you would make next time.
