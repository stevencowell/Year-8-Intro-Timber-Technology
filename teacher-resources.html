EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

Participation evidence, not invented assessment.

The modules, PowerPoint-derived web activities, Busy Work and folio collect learning evidence through device-local autosave, a self-contained HTML submission export and printable records. The HTML file is suitable for an intentional Google Classroom attachment but is not uploaded automatically. The course does not invent or imply formal dates, marks, weightings, rubrics, submission routes, competency decisions or notification status. Teachers must set those through approved local processes.

ACTION REQUIRED BEFORE PRACTICAL DELIVERY

Local controls remain local.

  • Current SOPs and risk controls: confirm approved procedures, supervision, permissions, PPE and emergency arrangements for the actual workshop.
  • Plan-controlled production: confirm materials, joints, sequence, tolerances, tools, machinery and finish from the original plan and teacher directions.
  • Slide 19 conflict: keep its 360/375 conflict excluded from dimensional teaching.
  • Optional decorative process: confirm whether laser cutting or engraving is available and approved; the course supplies no settings.
  • Assessment and submission: provide any approved task notification, dates, marks, weighting, rubric, adjustments and submission route separately.
