TEACHER RESOURCES

Year 8 Footstool Program

Teacher-developed, source-grounded planning materials for this 20-week elective.

Program status

Teacher-developed Program for local review — not NESA approved. This is a Year 8 elective, not Technology Mandatory. The 2025 Industrial Technology Stage 4 Timber material is used as planning context; local program, safety and assessment decisions remain school controlled.

EVIDENCE PACKAGE

Use evidence, not invented marks

The course includes student checks, two written prompts in every learning section, printable folio evidence and ten purposeful Busy Work activities. This is an evidence collection package, not a formal task notification. Set dates, marks, weighting, descriptors, submission route and feedback process through your local school process.

LOCAL RELEASE CHECK

Confirm before practical delivery

  • Current school SOPs and permissions for all tools and machinery.
  • Construction sequence, materials, joints, finish and any settings against the original plan and local procedures.
  • Resolution of the plan/deck dimensional conflict before dimensions are taught from the slide deck.
  • Approved assessment and submission arrangements.
  • Approved reuse treatment for the named supporting tool and material visuals.
